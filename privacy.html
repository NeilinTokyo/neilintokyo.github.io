Neil AIOS

Privacy and data use

Neil operates Neil AIOS as a private personal assistant utility. This policy describes its Google integration as of October 2, 2026.

Information accessed and its purpose

Each Google account grants separate consent. Read-only Gmail, Calendar, and Drive permissions allow access to messages, calendar information, and files relevant to requested tasks. Google identity information identifies the account in use.

The information supports finding records, summarizing content, reviewing schedules, and preparing plans. Current permissions do not allow sending email or modifying Google events or files.

Storage and sharing

Google credentials are encrypted and stored locally, separately from the workspace and its backups. Retrieved information may be processed by the AI assistant service Neil selects, such as OpenAI or Anthropic, to perform the requested task. Those providers' terms and the selected account's privacy settings also apply.

Saved notes and summaries may be retained in Neil's private workspace, synchronized through OneDrive, and backed up to a private GitHub repository. Sensitive records are excluded from tracked shared context. Google credentials are excluded from these backups.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. It is not shared with unrelated parties unless Neil instructs a disclosure for the requested task. Neil AIOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Neil controls which notes are retained; there is no fixed retention period. Access can be revoked through Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access and does not automatically remove previously saved records.

For questions or requests to delete retained information, contact neil@tokyoacademics.com. Deletion may also require removal from synchronized copies and backups.

This website

This site has no sign-in form, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
